Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

The SED Poonch application ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide secure access and official grievance submission features, we collect the following information:

  • Phone Numbers: We securely verify your phone number using the Truecaller SDK to ensure the authenticity of Head of Institutions (HOIs) and staff members.
  • School Data: We collect and verify UDISE codes and school names to link your profile to the correct institution.
  • User-Provided Information: Names, subjects, and media attachments submitted voluntarily through the public grievance system.

3. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is strictly used for official administrative purposes within the School Education Department (SED) Poonch, J&K. This includes verifying identities for school dashboards, authorizing account recovery, and processing official grievances sent directly to the CEO's office.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Phone numbers verified via Truecaller are used solely for authentication within the app. Grievance data is transmitted securely via email directly to the official CEO Poonch inbox.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: ceopoonchweb@gmail.com
